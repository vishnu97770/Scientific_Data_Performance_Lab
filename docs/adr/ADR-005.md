# ADR-005: Memory measurement (amended 2026-10-13)

**Status:** Accepted - amended by user decision 2026-10-13

## Context

Original: store raw `ru_maxrss` and a post-import baseline. Experiment (research-notes section 7.1) and two independent researcher probes showed a child's `ru_maxrss` can equal the parent's peak at launch while the child's `VmHWM` is independent.

## Alternatives considered

`ru_maxrss` only; psutil sampling; tracemalloc.

## Decision

Store as separate raw fields: worker `ru_maxrss_kib`, `vm_hwm_kib`, `vm_rss_baseline_kib` (VmRSS after imports, before the operation), and the runner's `parent_ru_maxrss_kib_at_launch`. Flag `maxrss_floor_suspect` when `ru_maxrss` does not exceed the parent's launch value. Any `vm_hwm - vm_rss_baseline` figure is labelled `estimated_workload_rss_kib` and is never presented as exact workload memory. Document KiB units, the inheritance effect and Linux-only limits. Keep the runner small (no held arrays; `report` imported lazily).

## Consequences / trade-offs

Stdlib only; more fields; a Phase 3 pytest reproduces the inheritance effect with a large margin. Not a heap or allocator measurement.
